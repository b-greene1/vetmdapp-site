VetMD keeps your dog's or cat's records, visits and reminders together - and helps you think through symptoms before you call the vet.

Built for the things owners actually forget

Not another reminder app. VetMD holds the record a vet would ask for, and makes it easy to answer "when was his last rabies shot?" in two taps.

Every record in one place

Breed, weight, microchip and rabies tag, logged visits, allergies, diet notes and vaccination history - the whole file, on your phone.

Reminders before things lapse

Rabies, distemper, heartworm, dental, flea and tick - set a date once and VetMD tells you before it comes due, not after.

Talk through symptoms

Describe what you're seeing and get plain-language guidance on what warrants a vet visit and how soon. It is not a diagnosis, and it always says so.

Vets and pet stores nearby

Find clinics and stores around you, call them from the app, and save the ones you trust so they're one tap away next time.

VetMD is not veterinary advice.

The app provides general information and record-keeping tools. It does not diagnose conditions or replace examination by a licensed veterinarian. If your pet is in distress, contact your vet or an emergency animal hospital immediately.
